About our community

About Our Community

Korean and Global Women’s Meetings Place is an international community created to bring women together across countries and cultures.

We believe that every woman has a story worth sharing. Through friendly conversations, cultural exchange, and shared life experiences, we hope to create meaningful connections and friendships that cross borders.

Our community welcomes women aged 18 and over, regardless of nationality, language, or cultural background.

From Korea to the world · A community built on listening and respect

Our purpose

Our Mission: Connecting Hearts Across Borders

Our mission is to connect Korean women with women around the world by creating a safe, welcoming, and supportive environment where women can:

Hearts across borders
  • 01 Share their life experiences.
  • 02 Exchange cultures and traditions.
  • 03 Express their thoughts and emotions.
  • 04 Build international friendships.
  • 05 Listen to and understand one another.
  • 06 Encourage and support each other.
